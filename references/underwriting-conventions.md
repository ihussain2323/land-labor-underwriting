# Underwriting conventions from prior reviews

Use these observations for an early screen when the new deal supports them. Cite the current source or the applicable comparable in the private deal record. Keep the original meeting references and workbook versions with the deal team.

**Deal lead review, November 10, 2026.** After the 235 East 44th Street and Quincy/Vanderbilt first passes, the deal lead set the items marked *(Nov 2026)* as the baseline. They replace the earlier precedents on new screens, for single assets and portfolios alike. A deal-specific instruction or a corroborated subject fact still overrides them for the units and dates it covers.

| Field | Observed treatment | What to verify on the new deal |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition and hold | *(Nov 2026)* 60-month hold is the baseline. Earlier screens used a first-of-month acquisition and a four-year hold. One deal already under LOI had a three-to-five-month estimated close. | Use the LOI or purchase agreement and document the deal stage. Choose a closing date explicitly. Confirm loan maturity separately. When in-place or assumed debt matures before the sale, model the refinance (see Debt). |
| Leasing and renewals | Prior reviews used 70% renewal probability. | Tie current occupancy to the acquired units on the rent roll. Set projected leasing from the vacancy assumption through the exit and forward year. |
| Rent growth | *(Nov 2026)* Market-rate units grow 3%. Rent-stabilized units grow 0%. Each layout's growth is the rent-dollar-weighted blend of the two. Earlier, a comparable used 2.75% and a later review selected 3% for all units. | Record ZIP rent and value research separately. Classify each unit's regulatory status from the rent roll, registrations and leases. Treat stabilized increases from an adopted RGB order as a sensitivity. |
| Bad debt | *(Nov 2026)* 0.5% in every year (Asset Inputs row 27), separate from vacancy and collections. | Check that the OM or seller vacancy allowance does not already include bad debt. Use an actual write-off ledger when supplied. |
| Operating expenses | Start with seller actuals. For missing categories use a comparable residential expense per unit, less costs already booked for the same service. Grow included dollar expense lines by 3% per year unless documented otherwise. | Show the seller, adjusted and comparable cases on the same revenue and debt basis. Tie monthly T-12 detail to the statement. Report trailing NOI only with sufficient revenue and expense evidence. |
| Personnel | *(Nov 2026)* About $200,000 a year for two FTEs at takeover, brought down to $150,000 as operations mature. Screens hold $200,000 for Years 1 and 2 and step to $150,000 in Year 1 dollars from Year 3 (Asset Inputs G175), then grow 3%. Earlier screens used the OM payroll per unit. | Use the seller payroll schedule and staffing plan when available. For a portfolio, apply the cost to the portfolio and allocate it by units in standalone models. Confirm FTE count with the deal lead for buildings well outside about 40 to 100 units. |
| Turnover / make-ready | *(Nov 2026)* Use the formula from the 515 Ninth (Asset Inputs G151) and 56N9 (H159) models: per unit, the average of $2,250 × annual turnover (1 − renewal rate) and $0.28 × rentable SF ÷ units. In this master G151 is `=((2250*(1-$E$26))+(0.28*$J$6/$N$11))/2`. 56N9 cites $2,250 per turn for paint, plaster and patch work, labor and cleaning. | Check seller R&M for cleaning, painting and appliance costs already booked. Disclose overlap; keep the formula unless the deal lead approves a reduction. |
| Other income and renovation | *(Nov 2026)* Included other income grows 3% into Year 1 (column E of rows 133–139) and in later years. Base cases require collections, billing support or a documented renovation plan and achievable rent premium. | Exclude unsupported receipts and premiums from the base. Show the basis and growth of included income. |
| Fees and reserves | A reviewed case used 3% of effective gross income for property management and 0.5% for asset management below NOI. Prior screens used replacement reserves of $250 per unit with 3% growth. | Verify the agreement, selector, expense base and placement relative to NOI. |
| Buyer and selling costs | Two NYC precedents used 3% buyer costs. Itemized budgets varied. Prior reviews used 4% selling costs. | Use a deal-specific budget when available. Reconcile tax, legal, diligence, reserves and debt fees without overlap. |
| Partner equity | Five of seven distinct precedents used 5% GP equity. The others used 10% and 20%. | Use the JV agreement when available. Apply the selected share to peak equity, then check GP and LP dollar contributions and distributions. |
| Debt | *(Nov 2026)* An assumable loan carries a 0.5% assumption fee, entered as the assumed facility's loan cost (Transaction Inputs row 41). One acquisition screen used 65% of price, 5.50% fixed interest and a 1.25% upfront fee. Another used 48 months of interest-only payments. The deal lead cited recent lender quotes of 5.39% to 5.85% and about 0.5% as a fair debt broker fee. | Use lender terms when available. Confirm the purchase period receives the proceeds, each fee occurs once and the model repays the balance at the intended date. Month 0 funded acquisition debt in the reviewed master. An older in-place debt model used month 1. When the assumed loan matures before the sale, set Loan 1's repayment source to Loan 2, fund Loan 2 in the maturity month and repay it at sale. Without a quote use 5.50% fixed IO and a 1.25% fee, and show the IRR with the loan extended at its coupon as a sensitivity. |
| Exit and taxes | *(Nov 2026)* 5.25% exit cap, reflecting higher interest rates. Earlier, a reviewed case used a provisional 5% exit cap. A tax-benefit deal required a dated transition to full taxes and a separate rent surcharge scenario. | Support exit pricing for the subject. Model a known tax increase even in the zero-surcharge case. Document eligibility, collection and timing before recognizing surcharge income. |

Start the investment review with acquisition price per unit and rentable square foot, entry NOI and cap rate, cash-on-cash yield, dated IRR and the LP check. Trace results through funding, holding period and exit. Check that beginning invested equity rolls from the prior ending balance for MFFO. Inspect dashboard links and the selected waterfall. In one master version, four formula errors in Transaction Inputs L56:L59 belonged to an inactive promote branch. Recheck dependencies in the actual deal copy.
