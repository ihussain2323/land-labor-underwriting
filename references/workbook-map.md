# Mapping the Excel template

The [machine-readable map](../assets/master-map.json) describes the reviewed Land Labor master. It lists candidate inputs and read-only controls, grouped by worksheet. Confirm the deal copy’s layout before a write. For example, GP share is Transaction Inputs P25 in this master and Q26 in an older benchmark workbook. That workbook uses an itemized cost formula where the master accepts a percentage. A repeated label such as Market Rent Growth must be resolved under its unit type. Different building classes and workbook generations can add rows, years or retail and waterfall sheets.

For a shifted sheet, locate the section by label, then the period, unit type, loan or selector heading. Inspect the formula that consumes the candidate input and its number format, validation and input style. Copy the map to the deal working folder, replace addresses and anchors, and inspect that copy. For an alternate model with its own pro forma, construct the equivalent mapping for its own structure. An unresolved binding stays in the manual work queue while other sections proceed. Conditional write plans contain scalar candidates; inspect remaining controls, T-12 lines, source formulas, scenario switches and summary repairs individually before applying them.
