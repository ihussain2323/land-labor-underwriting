# Conditional underwriting and checks

**Rent and unit match.** Build one row per acquired unit, with the unrounded area and signed or scheduled monthly rent. Preserve conflicting source flags. Group separately by use, unit type and regulation where it changes collectible rents. If a vacant unit carries scheduled rent, retain it in scheduled gross rent when that's the seller's convention, then deduct its downtime once. Report physical, economic and underwritten occupancy on their own bases. Confirm that concessions, bad debt and loss to lease do not each duplicate the OM’s combined allowance. A broad market forecast needs its geography, issue date and year of overlap.

**Post-COVID research.** Retrieve current official Zillow ZIP ZORI and ZHVI CSVs and document series definition, geography, release, currency/units and as-of date. Select an appropriate complete post-COVID baseline, after May 2023 when excluding the US emergency period. If latest common month is September 2026, start with September 2023 and average the September 2024, 2025 and 2026 YoY changes. Do not include the change into September 2023. Retain negative changes. Compare a dated ZIP or metro forecast where available, identifying horizon and coverage. A metro projection is a separate source. Use the selected underwriting growth in unit-type rows; neither the historical value index nor a house-price forecast sets the exit cap.

**Expense comparisons.** Rebuild T-12 expenses by month and tie them to the statement before underwriting. Benchmark at a common scope, unit count and tax regime. For a missing bucket, annual adjustment = comparable residential dollars per unit × subject units minus comparable costs already booked in the ledger. A negative result signals overlap to resolve. Do not mechanically add negative allowances. Keep taxes, property management and below-NOI fees separate. Check elevator, painting, insurance, payroll, utilities and condo charges across buckets. Show seller, gap-adjusted and comp cases on the same revenue and debt basis. Identify a true T-12 total separately from annualized or blended operating estimates.

**Expiring tax benefit or income restriction.** Identify the applicable property interest, tax lot, bill, assessment, statutory or contractual dates and ownership percentage. Use current official sources when legal rules or collectible amounts are material, and record documentary uncertainties. Model costs independently of proposed rent upside. For a dated tax reset, weight the current and full bills over months (or exact dates when material). Verify when full taxes begin and avoid growing the step twice. Keep the known full-tax change active even in the no-surcharge case.

For a surcharge, begin with eligible units and the documented historical rent base, riders, notices, registrations, lease dates and billing records. A broker’s calculation is a gross illustration until supported. Build a zero-collection base and requested rate cases using eligible rent × applicable cumulative percentage × collectible fraction of year. Treat a 2.2 percentage-point increment as an increment on the supported base, without compounding an 11% uplift on current income. Unit eligibility may shrink on turnover, deregulation or conversion. Apply vacancy/collections, management and other affected fees once. Show incremental gross rent, NOI, cash after debt and return by year. Capitalize only documented continuing exit income. If only a gross OM number exists, a proportional sensitivity on its implied basis is an illustration with explicit eligibility and start assumptions. Save the base case active. Record any difference between the upper bound in the offering memorandum and the case requested by the deal lead.

**Debt and partner capital.** Size acquisition debt from lender terms, a supported fixed amount or the selected screening LTV. Confirm numerator and denominator. Verify funding, interest-only months, amortization, day count, costs at funding and scheduled exit payoff. For in-place, floating, partial funding or refinance cases, reconcile each beginning balance + draws – principal = ending balance, fee at actual funding, rate resets and payoff before recognizing distributable cash. A refinance changes debt proceeds, costs and equity distributions. Compare initial sources with initial uses and total sources with total uses. For a JV, tie per-partner equity and distributions to the actual timing of pref, return of capital, promote and sale. A disabled or missing waterfall requires a documented pro rata screen until a compatible calculation and terms are available.

**Template defects discovered on the first building.** In one reviewed copy, moving an acquisition loan to month 0 put it in purchase sources and uses, and the native maturity formula then tied. Restore or change formulas only after tracing that specific defect. For MFFO in this version, carry the previous ending invested equity into the next beginning balance. Do not use annual cash flow as the invested equity denominator. Trace additional capital events on more complex structures. Inspect project IRR and GP/LP returns using the actual dated periods. Verify the discrepancy if an Excel cash-flow date differs from the documented closing date. Check dashboard loan labels, one-bedroom links, old forecast years, external research labels, tenant and replacement-cost examples, zero-spend divisions and uncalculated sensitivity rows. A full formula-error scan also requires classifying dependency and active/inactive case; many numeric reference mistakes never produce an Excel error.

**Handoff and second-building test.** Keep the property copy, source register, assumptions and review items in the deal’s working folder. Tell the deal lead what drives the entry yield, loan coverage, GP check and return, what was estimated, and which evidence would change the bid. State material limitations even if inactive sample errors remain. On the next property count applicable required inputs, inputs correctly completed before review, active errors, and material corrections after review. Count unsupported guesses as unresolved. Keep the convention changes the deal team confirms with their scope and exceptions. The target of 80% refers to correctly completed first-pass work and needs measurement on new deals.

**Optional calculation helper.** Run `python scripts/cases.py deal.json` for a first-of-month acquisition. It returns monthly amounts for the hold and one forward year. Supply any of the following as the value of an evidenced decision. All date intervals include the start and exclude the end.

- `expense_gaps` is a list of entries with category, benchmark_per_unit and already_in_seller. Output is the incremental annual allowance by category.
- `tax_schedule` may contain a bills list. Each entry has from, until and annual (the tax bill in dollars for the acquired interest). Cover the full horizon with continuous bills or expressly dated forecast assumptions. Mid-month changes are day-weighted within the month.
- `surcharge_cases` is a list of cases with name, collection_fraction, management_rate and periods. Each period has from, until, eligible_annual_rent and rate. Construct consecutive intervals for changes in eligibility; combine simultaneous cohorts on their eligible historical bases. Gaps imply zero. The helper reports gross and incremental operating NOI under the stated management and collection treatment. In Excel add any other affected costs, below-NOI fees and debt effects, then recalculate returns and save the base case. A first-year forecast or illustrative legal ceiling alone does not support continuing exit income.
